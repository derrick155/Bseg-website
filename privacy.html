Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Black Summit Enterprise Group LLC handles information submitted through its website and business-service workflows.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide through our forms, such as business and contact information, business-operating details, funding requests, Stack Finder responses, and merchant-processing workflow information. We do not ask for passwords, full payment-card numbers, or bank-login credentials through the Business Solutions forms.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to requests, evaluate which Black Summit service or business-solution category may be relevant, operate and improve the website, maintain records, prevent misuse, and communicate with you where permitted.

Funding and partner referrals

If you request business funding, information may be reviewed and, when appropriate, shared with funding providers or service providers involved in processing your request. For Business Solutions partner referrals, a partner-specific option is only shown after Black Summit activates that partner. We do not automatically transmit merchant statements or other sensitive files through the public partner-routing flow.

Website storage and attribution

The site may store a non-sensitive business identifier and attribution information such as landing page, referral source, and UTM campaign values in your browser. This helps connect a request with the source that generated it. We intentionally avoid persisting your form contact details in browser local storage.

Service providers

We use service providers to host the site, receive form submissions, and support business operations. Those providers process information for us under their own terms and security practices. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, Black Summit, or others.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, business recordkeeping, legal obligations, dispute resolution, and fraud prevention. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete information we control, subject to legal and recordkeeping requirements. Depending on where you live, applicable privacy law may provide additional rights. Contact us so we can evaluate and respond to your request.

Children

Our services are intended for business users and are not directed to children under 18.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to info@blacksummitenterprisegroup.com or mailed to Black Summit Enterprise Group LLC, 130 W Pleasant Ave #275, Maywood, NJ 07607.
