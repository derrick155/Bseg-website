The Process

Simple. Fast.
Straightforward.

01

Submit Your Application

Fill out our short credit application. Takes about 5 minutes. We just need basic business information to get started.

02

We Review Program Fit

We review your information against current provider criteria and, where appropriate, submit your file for provider review. Submission does not mean approval.

03

Review Your Offer

If a provider approves the application, we present the provider’s offer and required disclosures so you can decide whether it works for your business.

04

Complete the Provider Process

If you accept an approved offer, you complete the provider’s documentation and verification process. Funding timing varies by product, provider, and file completeness.

What to Expect After You Apply

Once we receive your application, we review the basic information and let you know what additional documentation may be needed for provider review.

  • We review your application and reach out to collect any additional documents needed
  • Where appropriate, your file is submitted to a funding provider based on current program criteria
  • Submission does not guarantee approval, an offer, funding amount, or specific terms
  • If approved, we present the provider’s offer and required disclosures for your review
  • You decide whether to proceed; Black Summit cannot accept, decline, or bind a provider on your behalf
  • If you accept, funding timing depends on provider verification, documentation, and product requirements

WHAT YOU'LL NEED

Application · last 3 months of business bank statements · debt sheet if applicable. Month-to-date statements, credit-card processing statements, or additional supporting documents may also be requested.
